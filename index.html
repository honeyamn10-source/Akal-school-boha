About the School

A Legacy of Education
Since 2000

Akal School Boha was established in 2000 and is managed by a private trust. Located in urban Boha, Budhlada block of Mansa district, Punjab. The school provides quality Punjabi-medium education from Pre-Primary through Class 12 under PSEB.

We build character with moral values rooted in our proud Punjabi culture, following the teachings of our Gurus to guide every student toward a better future.
